XEsync Rowing / Connection guide

Connect your XEBEX rower
over Bluetooth.

The Smart Console 2.0 on a XEBEX Air rower broadcasts your rowing data over Bluetooth. Here is how to get it onto an Android phone with the free XEsync Rowing app, and what to check when the rower does not show up.

For XEBEX Air rowers with the Smart Console 2.0 · Android 6.0 and later

01 Step by step

  1. Install XEsync Rowing.

    Get the free app from Google Play.

  2. Create your account.

    Create a free account, confirm your email address and sign in. This is what lets the app keep your workout history.

  3. Wake the console.

    Press a button on the Smart Console 2.0 or pull the handle so the console is on. Make sure Bluetooth is turned on in your phone.

  4. Scan from the app.

    Start the scan in the app and allow the Bluetooth permission when Android asks. Your rower appears in the list; tap it to connect. You do not need to pair it in Android’s Bluetooth settings.

  5. Row.

    Start rowing. Pace, power, stroke rate and distance appear on your phone, and the session is saved when you finish.

02 If the rower does not show up

The scan finds nothing

Check that the console is awake: its screen should be on. Pull the handle a few times, then scan again. Stay within a couple of metres of the rower while scanning.

Android asked for a permission and I refused

The app cannot scan without it. Open Android’s settings for XEsync Rowing, allow the Bluetooth (“Nearby devices”) permission, then scan again. On older Android versions, Bluetooth scanning also requires the Location permission and location to be switched on.

Another app or device was connected to the console

A Bluetooth console usually talks to one app at a time. Close any other rowing app, or disconnect the other phone or tablet, then scan again.

It connected, but no numbers are moving

The console only sends data while you row. Take a few strokes. If nothing changes, close the app, wake the console again and reconnect.

I have a different console

XEsync Rowing is built for the Smart Console 2.0. Other XEBEX consoles have not been confirmed. Send your console model and what you are seeing, and we will help you work through it.
