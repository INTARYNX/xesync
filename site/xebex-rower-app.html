01 What you get

Live metrics.

Pace per 500 m, power in watts, stroke rate, distance, calories and time, large enough to read from the seat. Heart rate appears when the console supplies it.

A rower on the water.

An animated rower follows your strokes across a changing sky, so there is more to look at than a row of numbers.

Every workout saved.

Sessions are saved when you stop, even offline. Review your history, weekly and monthly totals, and how pace and power changed during a workout.

02 Compatibility

Built for the
Smart Console 2.0.

XEsync Rowing is built for XEBEX Air rowers fitted with the Smart Console 2.0 and its Bluetooth rowing data connection. Other XEBEX consoles and rowing machines have not been confirmed.

04 Good to know

Is there an app for XEBEX rowers?

Yes. XEsync Rowing is a free Android app for XEBEX Air rowers with the Smart Console 2.0. It connects over Bluetooth, shows your pace, power, stroke rate and distance live, and saves every workout to your history.

Is the app free?

Yes. It is free, has no ads, and its source code is published under the MIT license. It is an independent project built by a XEBEX owner, not an official XEBEX product.

Are there other apps that work with XEBEX rowers?

Yes, some third-party rowing apps list XEBEX rowers as compatible. XEsync Rowing focuses on one thing: showing what your Smart Console 2.0 measures and keeping a history of it, for free.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not currently. XEsync Rowing is available for Android 6.0 and later. The code is open, and an iPhone port is an open invitation.
