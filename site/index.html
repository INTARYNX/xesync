06 Frequently asked

Which Xebex rowers and consoles does XEsync support?

XEsync is built for Xebex Air rowers with the Smart Console 2.0 and its Bluetooth rowing data connection. Compatibility with other Xebex consoles or rowing machines is not confirmed.

Is the Smart Console 2.0 required?

Yes. XEsync connects over Bluetooth to the data the Smart Console 2.0 exposes. Other console versions have not been tested.

Which Android versions does XEsync support?

XEsync supports Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) and later.

Why does XEsync need Bluetooth permissions?

XEsync uses Bluetooth to scan for and connect to your Xebex console's Smart Console 2.0, and to receive live rowing data — pace, power, stroke rate and distance — while you row.

Does XEsync show my heart rate?

Heart rate appears automatically when your Xebex console broadcasts it over Bluetooth, alongside pace, power, stroke rate and distance. Not every setup provides heart rate, so this depends on your console and any connected strap.

Does XEsync work offline?

Yes. If you're offline when a workout ends, XEsync saves it locally on your device and syncs it to your account automatically the next time you're online and signed in.

What happens to a session if my connection drops mid-workout?

The session keeps recording locally. When it ends, XEsync saves it to your device and syncs it to your account as soon as a connection is available.

Where is my workout data stored, and is it shared?

Synced workouts are stored on servers operated by Fournier Digital Services and are used only to show your own history. We do not sell, trade or share your personal data with third parties. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I delete a workout or my account?

Yes. Individual workouts can be deleted at any time from your session history in the app, and deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also delete your entire account from the account settings screen; your data is fully removed from our servers within 30 days.

Is an account required?

You'll want one to keep and review your workout history across sessions and on the web. Creating an account is free and only needs an email address.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not currently. XEsync is Android only today. It's MIT licensed, and porting it to iOS is an open invitation for anyone interested.

How can I report a compatibility or connection problem?

Email intarynx@gmail.com with your console model and what you're seeing, and we'll help you work through it.
